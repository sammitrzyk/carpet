Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Carpet Guys respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • ZIP code
  • Service address
  • Information about the services you are interested in
  • Any other information you provide when requesting a quote or contacting us

We may collect this information through our website, phone calls, text messages, and lead forms or advertisements on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Provide quotes or estimates
  • Schedule or provide requested services
  • Contact you regarding your service request
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain business and customer records
  • Improve our services and advertising

Text Messages and Phone Calls

If you provide your phone number, we may use it to contact you regarding your inquiry, quote, appointment, or requested services.

Where required, marketing or promotional text messages will only be sent with appropriate consent. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of promotional text messages at any time by replying STOP.

How We Share Information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers that help us operate our business, such as customer relationship management, communications, website hosting, advertising, and technology providers.

We do not sell your personal information.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable legal and business-record requirements.

Third-Party Platforms

When you interact with our advertisements or lead forms on third-party platforms such as Facebook or Instagram, those platforms may also collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact Carpet Guys using the contact information provided on our website.
